01.

Structural Engineering & Design

Advanced 3D numerical modeling, limit state design, ductile seismic detailing per IS 13920:2016, and statutory stability endorsements.

Discipline 01-A

RCC Structural Design

Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) constitutes the backbone of modern residential towers, commercial complexes, and institutional facilities. The Skytech deploys cutting-edge 3D FEM software (ETABS, SAFE, STAAD.Pro) to simulate multi-directional earthquake and wind responses under rigorous Limit State criteria.

Key Scope & Deliverables:

  • Mat/Raft, Pile-Cap and Combined Foundation engineering tailored to variable SBC.
  • Earthquake resistant shear wall cores per IS 1893:2016 and ductile detailing per IS 13920:2016.
  • Comprehensive Good-for-Construction (GFC) drawings with complete Bar Bending Schedules (BBS).
  • Deflection, vibration, and thermal expansion joint optimization.
Governing Codes: IS 456:2000 IS 1893:2016 IS 13920:2016 IS 875 Parts 1-5

Discipline 01-B

Steel Structural Design

Structural steel offers unparalleled strength, speed of erection, and column-free floor areas. We design industrial plant frames, pipe racks, conveyor bridges, heavy factory sheds, and commercial steel towers using limit state methods per IS 800:2007.

Key Scope & Deliverables:

  • Long-span roof trusses, heavy portal frames and crane gantry girders up to 30 MT capacity.
  • High-Strength Friction Grip (HSFG) bolted and full-penetration butt-welded joint connection design.
  • Base plate detailing, anchor bolt pull-out calculations, and reinforced concrete pedestals.
  • Detailed 3D fabrication drawings, cutting lists, and erection sequencing.
Governing Codes: IS 800:2007 IS 808 IS 875 (Wind up to 50 m/s) AWS D1.1

Discipline 01-C

MS Structures

Mild Steel (MS) structural frameworks deliver cost-effective, adaptable, and rapid solutions for industrial mezzanines, warehouse racking, secondary plant support framing, and architectural canopies per IS 800:2007.

Key Scope & Deliverables:

  • Heavy-duty mezzanine floor design with dynamic live-load and deflection control.
  • Overhead crane gantries, equipment support platforms, and conveyor walkways.
  • Comprehensive fabrication drawings, weld inspection specifications (DPI/UT), and anchor bolting details.
Governing Codes: IS 800:2007 IS 2062 IS 816 (Arc Welding)

Discipline 01-D

PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) Structures

Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEBs) optimize steel where stress is highest via tapered plate sections. Ideal for logistics parks, manufacturing warehouses, food processing facilities, and clear-span industrial complexes.

Key Scope & Deliverables:

  • Tapered column-rafter frames matched directly to bending moment envelopes.
  • Cold-formed continuous Z & C purlins engineered for wind uplift.
  • Clear spans up to 60+ meters for unobstructed logistics operations.
Governing Codes: MBMA Standards IS 800:2007 IS 801 (Cold-Formed)

Soil Testing and Report Submission

Scientific geotechnical soil investigation, rotary borehole drilling, in-situ Standard Penetration Tests (SPT), and certified Safe Bearing Capacity (S.B.C.) reports for fail-safe foundation design across Rajasthan and NCR.

Structural Audit, NDT, DT and Building Rehabilitation
