Service 01

RCC Structural Design

Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) constitutes the backbone of modern residential, commercial, and institutional developments. THESKYTECH deploys cutting-edge 3D FEM software (ETABS, SAFE, STAAD.Pro) to simulate multi-directional earthquake and wind responses under rigorous Limit State criteria.

Key Scope & Deliverables:

  • Mat/Raft, Pile-Cap and Combined Foundation engineering tailored to variable SBC.
  • Earthquake resistant shear wall cores per IS 1893:2016 and ductile detailing per IS 13920:2016.
  • Comprehensive Good-for-Construction (GFC) drawings with complete Bar Bending Schedules (BBS).
  • Deflection, vibration, and thermal expansion joint optimization.
Governing Codes: IS 456:2000 IS 1893:2016 IS 13920:2016 IS 875 Parts 1-5

Service 02

Steel Structure Design

Structural steel offers unparalleled strength, speed of erection, and column-free floor areas. We design industrial plant frames, pipe racks, conveyor bridges, heavy factory sheds, and commercial steel towers using limit state methods per IS 800:2007.

Key Scope & Deliverables:

  • Long-span roof trusses, heavy portal frames and crane gantry girders up to 30 MT capacity.
  • High-Strength Friction Grip (HSFG) bolted and full-penetration butt-welded joint connection design.
  • Base plate detailing, anchor bolt pull-out calculations, and reinforced concrete pedestals.
  • Detailed 3D fabrication drawings, cutting lists, and erection sequencing.
Governing Codes: IS 800:2007 IS 808 IS 875 (Wind up to 50 m/s)

Specialty Value Engineering

Building Cost Reduction (Approved Drawings)

Have your structural drawings already been approved, but contractor estimates exceed your budget? In 8 out of 10 projects, conventional drawings carry 15% to 25% redundant steel tonnage due to over-conservative assumptions or generic detailing. THESKYTECH re-evaluates the mathematical models and delivers optimized drawings with certified safety.

Proven Real-World Impact:

  • Average client savings: ₹8 to ₹25 per square foot of constructed area.
  • Elimination of unnecessary lap splices through standard bar cutting schedules.
  • Refined column sizing and slab reinforcement without changing architectural layouts.
  • Statutory re-endorsement and peer review seal included.
