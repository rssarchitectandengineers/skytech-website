ENGINEERING WORKFLOW

Our Proven 4-Step Engineering Process

A disciplined, quality-controlled methodology delivering precision drawings and zero site rework.

01
Consultation & Architectural Study

We review your architectural plans, soil test reports, site conditions and project objectives to determine the most efficient structural framing scheme.

02
Analysis & 3D Modeling

Finite element computer simulations in ETABS & STAAD.Pro for seismic, wind, dead and live loads ensuring maximum safety and code compliance.

03
Detailed Structural Drawings & BBS

Delivery of comprehensive Good-For-Construction (GFC) drawings, bar bending schedules (BBS), and material specifications that eliminate contractor ambiguity.

04
Site Inspection & Quality Vetting

On-site rebar reinforcement inspections before concrete pours to verify shuttering, cover blocks, and bar placements match approved designs.
