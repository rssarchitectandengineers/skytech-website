Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Policy

Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India • Effective: October 2026

1. Purpose & Scope

This policy outlines the commitment of TheSkyTech Structural Consultant (Headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India) as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). It applies to all digital personal data collected from prospective clients, developers, architects, and structural project partners.

2. Notice & Consent

When you request structural consultations, cost-reduction drawing audits, or project quotations, we collect personal data (Name, Phone Number, Email, Project Location, and Architectural Drawings) strictly based on your clear, affirmative consent. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Data Protection Grievance Officer.

3. Data Minimization & Engineering Purpose Limitation

Your data is processed solely for structural engineering design, stability assessments, soil report reviews, statutory building certifications, and official client communication. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or structural project data with third-party advertising brokers.

4. Client Rights (Data Principal Rights)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Summary of your personal and project data processed by TheSkyTech.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Rectification of inaccurate contact details and erasure of project data no longer necessary for statutory engineering recordkeeping.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Prompt resolution of any privacy or data handling concerns.

5. Data Security Measures

TheSkyTech implements robust organizational and technical safeguards, including encrypted communications, restricted internal drawing repositories, and access controls to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of structural models and client data.
