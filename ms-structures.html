Scope of MS Structural Engineering & Detail Capabilities

Mild Steel (MS) components provide cost-effective, adaptable, and robust structural solutions for secondary industrial framing, warehouse mezzanine floors, and machinery support platforms. The Skytech delivers structural calculations, vibration checks, and execution drawings.

1. Mezzanine Floor Engineering

Heavy-duty industrial mezzanine floor frames designed for dynamic storage loads, vibration dampening, and forklift movement.

2. Equipment Support Platforms

Elevated machinery platforms, tank foundations, and staircase towers with deflection limits governed by IS codes.

3. Pipe Racks & Conveyor Bridges

Multi-tier structural pipe racks, cable tray walkways, and conveyor galleries for processing plants and factories.

4. Weld Inspection & QA Standards

Weld qualification procedures (WPS), Non-Destructive Dye Penetrant (DPI), and Ultrasonic Testing (UT) guidelines.

Governing Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) & Global Codes: IS 800:2007 (Code of Practice for Steel Design) IS 2062:2011 (Hot Rolled Medium and High Tensile Steel) IS 816 (Metal Arc Welding for General Building) IS 1893:2016 (Seismic Load Criteria)
